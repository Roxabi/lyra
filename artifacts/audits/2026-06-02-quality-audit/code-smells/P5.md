### Summary
P5 (Adapters, agent_cmd) has 23 code-smell issues across 54 files. The most severe cluster is cross-platform duplication: Telegram and Discord adapters share nearly identical formatter, outbound send, and validation logic that is copy-pasted rather than abstracted. A secondary cluster is long functions in audio handling and bot-seed merge logic.
