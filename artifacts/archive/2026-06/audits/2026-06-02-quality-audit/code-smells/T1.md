### Recommendations
1. **Extract NDJSON builder** in `test_cli_streaming_parse.py` — replace 14 inline `json.dumps` blocks with a helper (e.g. `_ndjson_event(type, ...)`). Effort: low.
2. **Parametrize `TestSetParam`** — collapse 39 boundary-value methods into 2-3 `@pytest.mark.parametrize` tables (valid/invalid per field). Effort: medium.
3. **Parametrize `TestIsTransientError`** — collapse 23 one-liner methods into a single parametrized test with a `[(exception_type, expected), ...]` table. Effort: low.
4. **Split `TestModelConfig`** into `TestBackendValidation`, `TestFieldDefaults`, `TestEqualityHash` sub-classes. Effort: low.
5. **Extract shared fixtures** for `InboundMessage`, `_log_turn`, and `make_router` calls to reduce repetition in `test_audio_pipeline_tts.py`, `test_turn_store.py`, and `test_command_router_special.py`. Effort: low.
6. **Remove or migrate skipped v1 tests** — the 4 `@pytest.mark.skip` tests in `test_pool_streaming.py` and `test_audio_pipeline_tts.py` (stubs + skips) are dead code; either rewrite for v2 or delete. Effort: medium.
7. **Refactor `test_run_error_on_input_exception`** — split into 3 focused tests: exception re-raise, `RunError` content, and lifecycle ordering. Effort: medium.
