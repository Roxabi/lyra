### Recommendations
1. **Make model context limits configurable** (P1, effort: small) — Move `MODEL_CONTEXT_TOKENS` and `COMPACT_THRESHOLD` to `AgentConfig` or `HubConfig`; derive per-model instead of hardcoding 200k.
2. **Externalize user-facing strings** (P1, effort: medium) — Move `_SEND_ERROR_MSG`, `_CIRCUIT_OPEN_MSG`, and `SESSION_FALLTHROUGH_MSG` into `MessageManager` templates so they can be localized and overridden per-bot.
3. **Audit hardcoded constants for config exposure** (P2, effort: medium) — Batch-review constants in `outbound_errors.py`, `debouncer.py`, `circuit_breaker.py`, `_dispatch.py`, and `event_bus.py`. Expose those most likely to need tuning (e.g., `_BACKOFF_DELAYS`, `_MAX_ATTEMPTS`, `MAX_TRANSCRIPT_LEN`, `DEFAULT_DEBOUNCE_MS`) via `HubConfig` or environment overrides.
4. **Reduce complexity in `SttMiddleware` and `StreamingDispatch`** (P2, effort: medium) — Split `SttMiddleware.__call__` into smaller helper methods per outcome; extract dispatcher-queue vs direct-adapter branching in `StreamingDispatch.dispatch` into a dedicated router.
5. **Clean up backward-compat shims** (P2, effort: small) — Search the codebase for imports of `_DROP`, `_SESSION_FALLTHROUGH_MSG`, and `message_pipeline` (the module). If none found, remove the shim and aliases. If found, schedule deprecation.
6. **Address `ChannelAdapter` ISP split** (P2, effort: large) — Follow the docstring plan: split into `MessageReceiver` (inbound driver port) and `MessageSender` (outbound driven port) under `core/ports/`. This is architectural debt that impacts testability.
7. **Narrow defensive `type: ignore` catches** (P2, effort: small) — In `middleware_stt.py`, add a runtime `None` check for `hub._msg_manager` before the call, then remove the `type: ignore` comments.
8. **Review broad exception boundaries** (P2, effort: small) — In `session_lifecycle.py`, catch specific JSON/parsing errors instead of `Exception` in extraction loops; log and suppress only known failure modes.
