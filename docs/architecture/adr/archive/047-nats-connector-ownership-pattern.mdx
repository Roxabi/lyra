## Original Summary

Established the ownership pattern: lyra owns all `lyra.*` NATS subjects, wire contracts, and contract ADRs; satellite repos (voiceCLI, imageCLI, roxabi-vault) own the worker processes and confine subject literals to a single adapter module.
