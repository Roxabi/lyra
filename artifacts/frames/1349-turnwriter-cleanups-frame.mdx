## Problem

PR #1347 (TurnStore α-refactor, hub → writer-subscriber split, closed #1331) merged with 6 non-blocking review suggestions deferred via `/fix` walkthrough. Three of them are latent operational risks — Quadlet false-green (S1), silent NATS error swallowing (S2), private-API coupling that breaks on dep upgrade (S3) — and three are doc/test debt that erodes the value of the just-shipped wiring (S4, S5, S7). Cost-of-doing is low (~7 files, single domain, ~1 PR) and known; cost-of-carrying compounds on the next prod incident or `nats-py` bump.

Material drift surfaced by `/recheck`: #1370 already replaced `HealthCmd=pgrep` with `HealthCmd=grep -q turn-writer /proc/1/cmdline` across all `lyra-*` Quadlets. The S1 complaint stands — both forms are shallow process-presence checks — but the *premise wording* in the issue is stale. Frame must re-anchor S1 against current state.

  - Ops / on-call — beneficiary of S1 (real health signal) and S2 (logged warning vs silent restart loop).

## Premise Validity

**Success in 6 months:**
All 6 items applied. `lyra-turn-writer` reports HTTP-based health on `:8083` (S1), the consume loop survives NATS connection loss with logged warning before re-raise (S2), stream/consumer bootstrap uses only public `JetStreamContext` methods (S3), ACL matrix + `docs/architecture/messaging.mdx` document the `clipool-worker` exclusion from `lyra.turns.write` (S4), `lyra-data.volume` description names the writer-subscriber as `turns.db` writer (S5), and `test_on_resume_fn_wired_when_turn_publisher_present` has its strict assertion restored (S7). No regressions in #1331 TurnWriter subsystem.

**Failure in 6 months (falsifiable):**
Quadlet reports `lyra-turn-writer` healthy while the consume loop is in a dead state (no NATS pull, no writes to `turns.db` for >5 min) — observable via `/health` probe + `turns.db` row-count delta — AND/OR a `nats-py` minor upgrade breaks `stream_setup.py` silently (no test catches it) — observable via CI matrix on upstream bump.

**Simplest alternative:** Do nothing — leave the 6 items as logged review debt; revisit only on a real incident.
**Why not simplest:** S1 is a deploy-time false-green risk (already cost us once via #1370 — a Quadlet `HealthCmd` parsing bug shipped to prod). S3 is a silent-break risk on the next `nats-py` bump. S7 weakens regression coverage of the just-shipped #1331 wiring. The 6 items are small enough to ship together (~7 files, single domain), so cost-of-doing is lower than carry cost.
