### Recommendations (prioritized, with effort estimate)

1. **Anchor fire-and-forget session persistence tasks** (P1, ~15 min) — In `cli_pool_session.py`, store the `create_task` result in a `set[asyncio.Task[None]]` with a done-callback (same pattern as `self._audit_tasks` in `cli_pool_spawn.py`). This prevents unhandled exception loss and gives observability.

2. **Wrap blocking unlink calls in `asyncio.to_thread()`** (P2, ~10 min) — In `cli_pool_spawn.py` lines 162/182 and `cli_pool_worker.py` line 102, replace `Path(...).unlink(missing_ok=True)` with `await asyncio.to_thread(Path(...).unlink, missing_ok=True)`.

3. **Wrap TOML seeding I/O in `asyncio.to_thread()`** (P2, ~10 min) — In `agent_seeder.py`, call `_parse_toml(path)` via `await asyncio.to_thread(_parse_toml, path)` so `open()` + `tomllib.load()` do not block the event loop.

4. **Offload hot-reload checks to a thread** (P2, ~30 min) — In `agent.py`, wrap `_maybe_reload_config()` and `_maybe_reload_plugins()` bodies in `asyncio.to_thread()` or use `asyncio.gather` with `to_thread` for the individual blocking pieces. Alternatively, move the hot-reload trigger to a periodic background task instead of every `process()` call.

5. **Replace `asyncio.get_event_loop()` with `asyncio.get_running_loop()`** (P3, ~5 min) — In `cli_pool_lifecycle.py` lines 60 and 65.
