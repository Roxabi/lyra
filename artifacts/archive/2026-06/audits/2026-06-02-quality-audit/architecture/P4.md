### Recommendations (prioritized, with effort estimate)
1. **Extract `AuthStoreProtocol`** to `core/stores/auth_store_protocol.py` and switch `authenticator.py` to use it (medium effort, 1-2 files). This closes the last ADR-048 gap in auth.
2. **Switch `IdentityAliasStore` -> `IdentityAliasStoreProtocol`** in `authenticator.py` (low effort, 1 file). The protocol already exists; only the import path needs to change.
3. **Move `SessionTools` to `core/ports/` or `core/processors/`** and have `lyra.integrations.base` re-export it (medium effort, 2-3 files). Breaks the `core -> integrations` dependency.
4. **Relocate `SanitizedError`** to `core/messaging/` or `core/ports/` (low effort, 1 file). `lyra.transport` can re-export for backward compatibility. Aligns `core/CLAUDE.md` whitelist with `.importlinter`.
5. **Extract `close_reasoning_if_open()` guard** into a shared helper/mixin (low effort, 1 file). Reduces duplication in stream handlers.
