### Recommendations
1. **Replace asyncio.sleep with event-driven synchronization in integration tests** (P1, effort: medium) — In `test_hub_standalone.py`, `test_nats_bus.py`, and `test_nats_bus_multibot.py`, replace `asyncio.sleep()` waits with `asyncio.Event` or `asyncio.Queue` based synchronization. For `test_hub_standalone.py`, use an `asyncio.Event` that the Hub signals when `run()` is ready, or use `asyncio.wait_for` on a queue with a concrete timeout. For `test_nats_bus.py:344`, replace the 500ms sleep with an event-based approach or use `asyncio.wait_for` with a shorter timeout and a loop that checks `staging_qsize()`.

2. **Add fail-safe timeouts for asyncio.sleep(9999) patterns** (P2, effort: low) — Wrap the `consumer.start()` + `consumer.stop()` calls in `test_jetstream_audio_consumer.py` and `test_outbound_audio_delivery.py` with `asyncio.wait_for()` so that if cancellation fails, the test fails fast rather than hanging indefinitely.

3. **Use freezegun for time-based assertions** (P2, effort: low) — In `test_health.py:196` and `test_keepalive.py`, replace real `asyncio.sleep()` calls with `freezegun.freeze_time()` or a mock clock to make tests deterministic and faster.

4. **Scope Path.home patch to function level** (P2, effort: low) — Move `monkeypatch.setattr(Path, "home", ...)` from the class-level `test_health_detail_includes_adapters_key` into a fixture or ensure it is strictly function-scoped to avoid leaking to other tests.

5. **Add crash-recovery coverage for turn-writer** (P3, effort: medium) — Address the TODO markers in `test_writer.py` by adding tests for mid-batch crash recovery and JetStream redelivery scenarios, or remove the TODO if the decision is to test this at deploy/integration level.

6. **Review nonlocal patterns in test closures** (P3, effort: low) — In `test_nats_channel_proxy.py`, refactor the nested async functions with `nonlocal call_count` to use `MagicMock(side_effect=[...])` or a small class-based counter to avoid mutable state in closures.
