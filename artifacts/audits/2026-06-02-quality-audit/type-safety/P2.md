### Recommendations (prioritized, with effort estimate)
1. **Replace `object` with `Any` or a Protocol** (P1, 6 issues, ~30 min) — `object` in `command_router.py` and `command_config.py` (`session_driver`, `tools`) should be `Any` or a narrow Protocol. `object` is too broad and prevents any attribute access without casting.
2. **Remove `# type: ignore[arg-type]` in `agent_db_loader.py`** (P1, 1 issue, ~15 min) — The `effort` field is a `str | None` from DB, but `ModelConfig.effort` is `Literal["low", "medium", "high", "xhigh", "max"] | None`. Add a validation helper that converts the string to the Literal type, or use `typing.cast` after validation, instead of suppressing the error.
3. **Parameterize `dict`/`list`/`set` in builder/loader functions** (P2, ~15 issues, ~45 min) — `agent_builder.py`, `agent_db_loader.py`, `agent.py`, `command_loader.py`, and `command_router.py` have unparameterized generics. Most can be typed as `dict[str, Any]`, `list[str]`, `set[asyncio.Task]`, etc. This is a bulk find-replace with review.
4. **Parameterize `dict` in `cli_streaming_parser.py` internal handlers** (P3, 8 issues, ~20 min) — All `_handle_*` methods take `data: dict`. These parse JSON wire data, so `dict[str, Any]` is the correct annotation. Low priority because they are internal private methods.
