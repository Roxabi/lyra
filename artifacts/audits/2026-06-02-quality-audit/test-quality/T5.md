### Recommendations
1. **Move module-level subprocess calls to fixture** (`test_gh_shells.py` lines 42-46) — Effort: S. Replace `_SOCAT` / `_BSD_NC` module-level `subprocess.run` calls with a `@pytest.fixture(scope="session")` or `pytest_sessionstart` hook that sets a skip flag. This removes import-time side effects and keeps the skip logic intact.

2. **Replace `assert_called_once()` with `assert_awaited_once()` on all AsyncMock instances** (`test_gh_token_dispenser.py` lines 85, 116; `test_session_builder.py` lines 148, 182, 217) — Effort: XS. This closes the gap where calling an async mock without `await` would still pass the test.

3. **Add explicit assertions to no-op tests** (`test_llm_client_control.py:137`, `test_obs_base.py:111`, `test_obs_base.py:115`) — Effort: S. Verify observable state after the call (e.g., `assert client._lyra_sessions == {}` or `assert trace.ended_at is not None`) rather than relying on "does not raise" as the only pass condition.

4. **Replace real sleeps in daemon tests with `asyncio.Event` or deterministic synchronisation** (`test_gh_daemon.py` lines 131, 230) — Effort: M. Use an `asyncio.Event` or a callback-based gate that the daemon signals when the socket is bound, eliminating the polling loop and the 1 s bounded wait.

5. **Resolve or remove skipped placeholder test** (`test_classifier.py:316`) — Effort: S. Either implement the classifier heuristic for DEBT suggestions and enable the test, or delete the placeholder and track the work purely in the issue tracker (#1163).

6. **Tune `_wait_until` sleep granularity in typing NATS test** (`test_integration_nats.py:32`) — Effort: XS. Increase `asyncio.sleep(0.01)` to `asyncio.sleep(0.05)` or use `asyncio.Event` to reduce iteration count under slow runners.
