## Original Summary

Froze the wire contract for STT/TTS between lyra hub and voicecli satellite: subjects (`lyra.voice.stt.request`, `lyra.voice.tts.request`), payload schemas, `contract_version: "1"` field for forward compatibility, and heartbeat protocol.
