### Recommendations
1. **Extract JetStream provision helper** (P1, effort: S) — Create a shared `lyra.infrastructure.jetstream_provision` module with `ensure_stream(js, name, subjects, retention, ...)` and `ensure_consumer(js, stream, durable, filter, ...)` that centralizes the add/update + consumer_info/add_consumer pattern. This removes duplication across `turn_writer/stream_setup.py`, `outbound_audio/stream_setup.py`, and `audit/jetstream_sink.py`.

2. **Refactor TurnPublisher with generic `_publish_event`** (P1, effort: S) — Replace 5 repetitive `publish_*` methods with a single `_publish_event(kind: str, payload: TurnPayload, ...)` helper. The public methods become thin wrappers. This removes ~80 lines of duplication.

3. **Unify NATS subject sanitization** (P1, effort: XS) — Move `_safe_subject_token` / `_sanitize_key_part` to a shared helper in `packages/roxabi-nats` or `lyra.nats._sanitize`. Both callers use the same regex: replace `.`, `*`, `>` with `_`.

4. **Extract domain client base for `start`/`stop`/`is_available`** (P2, effort: S) — `NatsTtsClient`, `NatsSttClient`, and `NatsImageClient` share identical lifecycle boilerplate. Add a `DomainClientBase` mixin in `lyra.transport` or `lyra.nats` that holds `nc`, `pool`, and the lifecycle methods.

5. **Simplify `render_event_codec_registry.py`** (P2, effort: M) — `build_codec_registry` instantiates 17 `CodecBranch` objects with identical shape. Use a loop over a tuple of `(type, event_type, schema_version, is_done)` and call `_make_std_encode`/`_make_std_decode` inside the loop. This cuts ~100 lines of repetitive code.

6. **Extract SQLite transaction context manager** (P2, effort: S) — `pairing.py` and `identity_alias_store.py` both use raw `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` / `ROLLBACK` / `COMMIT` with `try/finally` style error handling. A `SqliteStore._transaction()` async context manager would remove the duplication and eliminate the risk of forgetting a `ROLLBACK`.

7. **Refactor `AgentStore.upsert` cache update** (P2, effort: XS) — Replace the 20-field manual `AgentRow(...)` reconstruction with `dataclasses.replace(row, updated_at=now)` if the row is immutable, or a `row.with_updated_at(now)` method. This removes ~40 lines of error-prone field-by-field copying.

8. **Split `TurnStore.connect` into phases** (P2, effort: S) — Separate DDL, index creation, migration, and backfill into private `_ensure_*` methods. Improves readability and testability.

9. **Fix `blobstore_adapter.aclose` encapsulation** (P2, effort: XS) — `HttpBlobStoreAdapter` should not access `HttpBlobStore` private attributes. Add a public `aclose()` method to `roxabi_blobs.HttpBlobStore` or use its existing `__aexit__` protocol.

10. **Add `_publish_json` helper in `nats_channel_proxy`** (P3, effort: XS) — Replace 11 occurrences of `json.dumps(..., ensure_ascii=False).encode("utf-8")` with a private `_publish_json` helper that also handles the `nats.errors.Error` catch+log pattern.
