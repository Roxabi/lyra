## Problem

The lyra→factory rename (epic #1671) renamed the **project/package** namespace to
`factory.` (PR #1669) but **deliberately left the NATS wire namespace on `lyra.*`** to keep
the cutover atomic. NATS subjects are exact-match strings: a publisher on
`factory.llm.generate.request` never reaches a subscriber still bound to
`lyra.llm.generate.request`. So the subject rename cannot land incrementally — every
publisher/subscriber, JetStream stream/consumer/KV name, and ACL grant must flip together
in one cutover window.

This issue covers the **roxabi-factory repo + the bundled `packages/roxabi-contracts/` SSoT**:
the 132 `lyra.*` subject literals/builders, the `LYRA_*` JetStream stream/KV identifiers,
the `deploy/nats/` ACL matrix + regenerated `auth.conf`, and the code-side residue
(`nats_bus.py` prefix, allowlist, inventory classifier). The 3 satellite repos
(voiceCLI/llmCLI/imageCLI) re-pin contracts `0.7.0` and cut over their own domains in
separate sub-issues — lockstep, but out of this worktree's scope.

## Who

- **Primary:** the NATS message bus and every process bound to it — `factory-hub`,
  `factory-{telegram,discord,clipool,turn-writer}`, plus the satellite workers
  (voice/llm/image). All must agree on the subject namespace or messages silently drop.
- **Secondary:** ops (maintenance-window cutover, stream recreation, secret/ACL deploy);
  downstream epic #1671 Phase 4 prod cutover which gates on this landing.

## Constraints

- **Breaking, lockstep across repos** — contracts `0.6.0`→`0.7.0`; satellites re-pin after publish.
- **JetStream stream names are immutable** — `LYRA_*`→`FACTORY_*` requires create-new+drain
  or recreate-on-empty, not in-place rename (key unknown for /analyze).
- **ACL is exact-subject** — `acl-matrix.json` + regenerated `auth.conf` must flip in the
  same deploy; frozen fixture `v3-pre-grant-group.json` stays untouched.
- **No prod impact until epic #1671 Phase 4** — `podman-auto-update.timer` disabled; this PR
  lands the code/contract change, the maintenance-window cutover is a separate step.
- Repo quality gates apply: `architecture_snapshot`, `doc_drift`, `import_layers`,
  `subject_literals_allowlist` (drop the `lyra.inbound` entry once renamed).

## Out of Scope

- Satellite-repo cutovers (voiceCLI#189, llmCLI#105, imageCLI#104) — separate sub-issues.
- Phase 4 prod cutover (data move `~/.lyra`→`~/.roxabi/factory`, Podman secret rename,
  `hosts.toml` role, re-enable auto-update timer) — no issue yet, epic #1671.
- "Lyra" persona / `lyra_default` / `bot_id "lyra"` — deliberately kept, not a wire subject.
- roxabi-vault (archived) and roxabi-cortex (design-phase, not yet a NATS peer).

## Premise Validity

**Success in 6 months:** All NATS peers (hub + voiceCLI/llmCLI/imageCLI) communicate on
`factory.*` subjects; zero `lyra.*` wire literals remain in roxabi-contracts / ACL matrix /
stream+KV identifiers; contracts published at ≥0.7.0; prod cutover completed with per-domain
heartbeat round-trips green.

**Failure in 6 months:** `lyra.*` and `factory.*` subjects coexist in prod past the cutover
window → dropped messages on subject mismatch; OR contracts still on `0.6.x` with `lyra.*`
literals after ≥3 release cycles (cutover perpetually deferred).

**Simplest alternative:** Dual-bind shim — publishers emit `factory.*` while subscribers bind
BOTH `lyra.*` and `factory.*` for a transition window (no maintenance downtime).
**Why not simplest:** The issue deliberately chose all-or-nothing. A dual-bind shim doubles
the ACL surface, cannot paper over the immutable JetStream stream names
(`LYRA_*`→`FACTORY_*` still needs recreate/drain regardless), and leaves dead `lyra.*`
bindings + an ambiguous wire state to clean up later → more total work. One coordinated
maintenance-window cutover is simpler net.
