### Recommendations (prioritized, with effort estimate)
1. **Replace `subprocess.run` with `asyncio.create_subprocess_exec`** in `git_ownership_probe.py` (P1). Wrap the probe in an async function and use `asyncio.create_subprocess_exec` with `asyncio.wait_for` instead of `subprocess.run`. Effort: 15 min.
2. **Run SQLite migration off the main event loop** in `bootstrap_stores.py` (P2). Wrap `_has_sentinel`, `_atomic_table_copy`, `_migrate_to_config_db`, and `_migrate_threads_to_discord_db` in `asyncio.get_running_loop().run_in_executor(None, ...)` so migration runs on a thread pool. Effort: 30 min.
3. **Use `aiofiles` or `asyncio.run_in_executor` for bootstrap file reads** (P3). Group the config/credentials/blobstore/lockfile reads into `asyncio.to_thread(...)` or `aiofiles` calls. Since they are small and one-time at startup, this is a low-priority polish. Effort: 45 min.
4. **Use `aiofiles` or `anyio.Path.mkdir` for `mkdir` calls** in async bootstrap functions (P3). Effort: 15 min.
