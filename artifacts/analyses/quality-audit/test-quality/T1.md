### Summary
Core unit tests show widespread sleep-based flakiness (57 instances across 14 files) and several missing-assertion tests that rely on "does not raise" without an explicit guard. Four tests are permanently skipped due to the stage-axis refactor (#1192 S3), creating a coverage gap. Mock overuse is high (879 references) but not critical.

### Recommendations
1. **Replace sleep-based polling with explicit synchronization** (P1, high effort ~2-3 days). In `OutboundDispatcher` tests, add an `asyncio.Event` or `asyncio.Queue` to the dispatcher that tests can await instead of `sleep(0.05)`. Same for `PoolManager` eviction tests and `TurnStore` backfill tests.
2. **Add explicit assertions to missing-assert tests** (P1, low effort ~2 hours). Add `assert True` or `pytest.does_not_raise` wrapper to `test_enqueue_accepts_outbound_message`, `test_emit_no_subscribers`, `test_idempotent_migration`, and `test_flush_nonexistent_pool_is_noop`.
3. **Unskip or delete skipped stage-axis tests** (P2, medium effort ~1-2 days). The 4 skipped tests in `test_pool_streaming.py` and `test_audio_pipeline_tts.py` reference #1192 S3. Either rewrite them for v2 streaming shape or remove them and add a tracking issue to prevent silent coverage rot.
4. **Reduce mock density in outbound dispatcher tests** (P2, medium effort ~1 day). Add at least one integration test that uses a real `LocalBus` or a fake adapter that records to a list, rather than 100% MagicMock coverage. The 879 mock references across the suite indicate a missing integration boundary.
5. **Extract inline mock classes to shared factories** (P2, low effort ~4 hours). `test_hub_circuit_streaming.py` redefines `SilentAdapter` and streaming agent stubs in every test; move them to `tests/core/conftest.py` or `tests/factories/`.
6. **Guard `object.__setattr__` bypasses** (P2, low effort ~2 hours). Replace `object.__setattr__(pool._observer, ...)` with a test double that exposes the hook via a public constructor or `typing.cast` of a Protocol, rather than monkeypatching internal state.
7. **Add cleanup to `asyncio.create_task` calls in tests** (P2, low effort ~2 hours). Use `try/finally` or `addCleanup` to cancel tasks created in `test_pool_advanced.py`, `test_pipeline_event_bus.py`, and `test_hub_circuit_streaming.py` so a failed assertion doesn't leak running tasks.
8. **Fix stale `BlobStorePort` RED comments** (P3, trivial effort ~5 min). Verify `lyra.core.ports.blobstore` exists and remove or update the comments in `test_blobstore_port.py`.
