### Rationale

Two principles converged:

1. **Slice-staging discipline.** Anything that would be rewritten in Slice 5 anyway (S2, S3, N6) belongs on the Slice 5 tracker, not in Slice 2's diff. Coexistence-window tests written specifically to be deleted in 2 PRs is negative ROI.
2. **Honest documentation over false promises.** S1's misleading docstring is the strongest pre-merge concern — it actively misleads future implementors. Fix the docstring now; defer the architectural relocation to Slice 5 where it lands naturally with v1 removal.

N7 (apply) and the SKIPs (N2-N5) reflect substantive review of reviewer claims: some findings were stylistic-only (N2, N4) or rejected on second look (N3, N5).

### Trade-offs

- Deferring S2/S3 leaves snapshot coverage that filters v2 rather than asserts it — a real coverage gap that only closes at Slice 5. The DEBT tag makes it visible but relies on human discipline.
- Fixing S1 docstring without wiring the seam keeps dead-code intent in the emitter for 3 more slices. A future reader who misses the DEBT comment may still trust the seam exists.
- Skipping N4 lets a misleading fixture key (`"error"` for soft-error) accumulate callers; harmless now, costs more to rename post-Slice-5.
