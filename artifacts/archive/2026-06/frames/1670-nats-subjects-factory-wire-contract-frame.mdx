## Problem

NATS subjects are exact-match strings. The lyra→factory migration (epic #1671) renamed the
package/CLI/env/container identity to `factory.*` (PR #1669) but **deliberately left the wire
namespace on `lyra.*`** to keep that PR non-breaking. The two namespaces are now split:
project/package = `factory.`, wire subjects = still `lyra.`.

This issue closes the gap: rename the wire contract `lyra.*` → `factory.*` across the
roxabi-contracts SSoT, the JetStream stream/KV identifiers, and the NATS ACL/auth config. A
renamed publisher (`factory.llm.generate.request`) will never reach a subscriber still bound
to `lyra.llm.generate.request`, so this **cannot land incrementally** — every
publisher/subscriber + stream/consumer/KV name + ACL grant flips in one coordinated cutover.

Observable impact today: scope re-verified intact at frame time — 90+ `lyra.*` literals in
contracts subjects, 93 `lyra.*` entries in `deploy/nats/acl-matrix.json`, 2 `lyra.inbound`
refs in `nats_bus.py`, and the four `LYRA_{OUTBOUND_AUDIO,AUDIT,TURNS,STATE}` stream/KV
identifiers all still present. Nothing is pre-done.

## Who

- **Primary:** the NATS peers — `factory-hub` + all adapters/workers that publish or subscribe
  on these subjects. A namespace mismatch = silent message loss (no error, just no delivery).
- **Secondary:** the satellite repos (voiceCLI #189, llmCLI #105, imageCLI #104) that re-pin
  roxabi-contracts 0.7.0 and cut over their domain subjects in lockstep. They are tracked by
  their own sub-issues and are **out of this worktree's scope** (see Out of Scope).

## Constraints

- **roxabi-contracts is the wire SSoT.** Subjects are `Literal[...]`-typed constants
  (`Literal["lyra.llm.generate.request"]`) precisely so pyright catches typos at check-time on
  an exact-match wire. The rename must preserve this typing — replace literal-for-literal, do
  not downgrade to plain `str`.
- **Breaking change → semver major-ish bump:** roxabi-contracts `0.6.0 → 0.7.0`; publish;
  satellites re-pin (their sub-issues block on the publish).
- **JetStream stream names are immutable.** `LYRA_OUTBOUND_AUDIO`, `LYRA_AUDIT`, `LYRA_TURNS`
  + KV bucket `LYRA_STATE` cannot be renamed in place — require create-new + drain, or
  recreate-on-empty, during the maintenance window. This is the main implementation unknown
  (→ /analyze).
- **ACL is generated.** `deploy/nats/acl-matrix.json` (`lyra.*` → `factory.*`, `owner:"lyra"`
  → `"factory"`), then regen `auth.conf` via `make nats-regen-specs`, update ACL spec sentinel
  blocks + `tests/scripts/fixtures/v3-current.json`. Leave frozen `v3-pre-grant-group.json`.
- **Code-side residue** in this repo: `nats_bus.py` `subject_prefix = "lyra.inbound"` →
  `factory.inbound`; drop the `lyra.inbound` entry from
  `scripts/subject_literals_allowlist.txt`; update `tools/code_inventory.py` subject classifier.
- **All-or-nothing cutover** happens in a maintenance window (hub + satellites stopped,
  streams/KV/ACL recreated, all deployed, restarted, smoke-tested). This issue lands the
  *code + contract* change; the prod cutover itself is epic Phase 4 (separate, no issue yet).
- ¬`--force` / ¬`--hard` / ¬`--amend`. File-length gate (300 SLOC), import-layers,
  doc_drift, architecture_snapshot all apply.

## Out of Scope

- **Satellite repos** (voiceCLI/llmCLI/imageCLI) — they re-pin + cut over in their own
  sub-issues *after* 0.7.0 publishes. Not edited from this worktree.
- **Phase 4 prod cutover** — data move, host secret rotation, stream recreation on the live
  server, re-enabling `podman-auto-update.timer`. Tracked separately under epic #1671.
- **roxabi-vault** (archived → no action) and **roxabi-cortex** (design-phase, not yet a NATS peer).
- **Kept deliberately** (NOT renamed): the "Lyra" persona, `lyra_default`, `bot_id "lyra"`,
  git author trailers, `lyra-*` Claude plugins, `roxabi/base*` images.

## Premise Validity

**Success in 6 months:** All NATS peers (hub + voiceCLI/llmCLI/imageCLI) communicate on
`factory.*` subjects; zero `lyra.*` wire literals remain in roxabi-contracts / ACL / stream-KV
identifiers; contracts published at ≥0.7.0; prod cutover completed with per-domain heartbeat
round-trips green.

**Failure in 6 months (falsifiable):** `lyra.*` and `factory.*` subjects coexist in prod past
the cutover window → messages dropped on subject mismatch; OR contracts still on 0.6.x with
`lyra.*` literals after ≥3 release cycles (cutover perpetually deferred).

**Simplest alternative:** Dual-bind shim — publishers emit `factory.*`, subscribers bind BOTH
`lyra.*` + `factory.*` for a transition window (no maintenance downtime).
**Why not simplest:** Dual-bind doubles the ACL surface, can't paper over immutable JetStream
stream names (`LYRA_*`→`FACTORY_*` still needs recreate/drain), and leaves dead `lyra.*`
bindings + an ambiguous wire state to clean up later → more total work. One coordinated
maintenance-window cutover is simpler net.

## Key Design Decision — namespace strategy

**Chosen: hardcoded literal-for-literal sweep** (issue as-written).

Rejected alternatives (recorded so the next rename doesn't relitigate):
- **Codegen the literals from a single `namespace=` source** — would kill future sweep cost
  and keep `Literal` typing, but adds a generator + regen gate. Deferred: renames should be
  rare; revisit as its own issue if they become frequent.
- **Runtime/config prefix** — DRYest, but `Literal[f"{ROOT}…"]` is illegal (loses pyright
  typo protection) and a config-driven wire root lets two deployed peers silently diverge.
  Rejected as an anti-pattern for exact-match subjects.
