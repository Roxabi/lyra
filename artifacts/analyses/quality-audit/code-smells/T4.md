### Recommendations (prioritized, with effort estimate)

1. **Split crash-recovery test into helpers / smaller tests** (P1, ~20 min) — Extract `_create_events`, `_inject_crash_handler`, `_assert_final_state` from `test_processed_events_table_dedupe_after_simulated_crash` or split into 3 focused tests (normal processing, crash leaves no row, replay succeeds).

2. **Extract shared test fixtures to conftest.py or `tests/integration/conftest.py`** (P2, ~15 min) — Move `_FakeTurnStore`, `_make_pool`, `_count_processed_events`, and `_now()` to a shared location to eliminate cross-file duplication.

3. **Parameterize triplicated stub functions in test_command_sessions.py** (P2, ~10 min) — Replace `_stub_vault_add`, `_stub_explain`, `_stub_summarize` with a single `_make_session_stub(command_name, usage_msg, response_template)` factory.

4. **Add `_make_proc` helper to test_systemctl.py and test_audio_converter.py** (P2/P3, ~10 min) — Follow the pattern already used in `test_vault_cli.py` and `test_web_intel.py`.

5. **Extract module-level `_wait_for_base_heartbeat` fixture** (P2, ~5 min) — Move the duplicated autouse fixture out of both test classes in `test_voice_routing.py`.

6. **Extract `_make_request` helper in test_voice_routing.py** (P2, ~5 min) — Factory that takes `trace_id` and `request_id` to remove 4 identical request dicts.

7. **Extract shared e2e setup helper in test_e2e_telegram_to_agent.py** (P2, ~5 min) — `_make_hub_and_adapter(provider_result_text)` to deduplicate the 6-line setup block.

8. **Move nested test functions to module level** (P2/P3, ~10 min) — `_crash_on_e4` in `test_crash_recovery.py` and `_resume_fn` occurrences in `test_on_resume_callback.py`.

9. **Remove dead code** (P2/P3, ~5 min) — Delete the unreachable block after `pytest.skip()` in `test_crash_recovery.py` and the unused `TextRenderEvent` / `ToolSummaryRenderEvent` stubs in `test_reasoning_e2e.py`.

10. **Loop event construction in test_crash_recovery.py** (P3, ~5 min) — Build e1-e5 with a list comprehension instead of 5 nearly identical blocks.
