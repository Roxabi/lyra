## Problem

`docs/architecture/adr/` contains **73 live ADRs** with **0 archived**, up from 37 when consolidation was first scoped (#1002, closed). Superseded decisions sit alongside active ones, multiple ADRs overlap thematically (NATS core, NATS contracts, Quadlet, architecture), and there is an active **ADR-017 number collision** (`017-coupling-hotspots` and `017-srp-violations` both exist). Navigability and signal-to-noise have degraded; new contributors and `dev-core` skills can't reliably resolve "the canonical ADR for X."

PR #1004 attempted this consolidation 72 commits ago and went stale; its bulk-move of ~27 archive relocations is likely still applicable as raw material but must be re-validated against six new ADRs (066–071) added since.

## Who

- **Primary:** Lyra contributors browsing `docs/architecture/adr/` to understand current decisions; agents (architect, doc-writer) that cite ADRs in plans/specs.
- **Secondary:** Cross-project consumers (roxabi-* repos that mirror the ADR pattern) using Lyra as the reference template.

## Constraints

- Pre-commit hooks must pass (lint, typecheck, file-length, folder-size, import-layers).
- No broken cross-references — every internal link in remaining live ADRs must resolve.
- Every archived ADR needs a `superseded_by` frontmatter pointer.
- `superseded_by` chains must be acyclic and terminate in a live ADR.
- Re-validate PR #1004 buckets against current state (six new ADRs since: 066–071); do not blindly replay them.
- Original buckets to re-audit:
  - NATS Core: 037, 040, 047, 062 → ADR-045
  - NATS Contracts: 044, 050 → ADR-049 (audit ADR-066 WorkerError envelope as candidate)
  - Quadlet: 053, 054, 056 → ADR-055 (audit ADR-068 SELinux `:z` policy as candidate)
  - Architecture: 048, 060, 061 → ADR-059
- ADR-017 collision: one of the two must be renumbered; all inbound links updated.

## Out of Scope

- Authoring new architectural decisions (this is a content reorg, not a design exercise).
- Migrating ADRs to a different format/tool (still MDX with frontmatter).
- Cross-repo ADR consolidation (other roxabi-* projects out of scope here).
- Building tooling/lints to enforce the live/archive split going forward — track separately if desired.
