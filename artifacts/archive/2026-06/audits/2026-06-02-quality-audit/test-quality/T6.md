### Recommendations
1. **Fix weak assertion** (effort: 5 min) — Add `assert "Something went wrong" in resp.content` or `assert resp.metadata.get("error")` to `test_send_returns_error_on_driver_error` in `tests/fixtures/test_agent_harness.py`.
2. **Eliminate module-level blob registry** (effort: 15 min) — Replace `_TEST_BLOB_REGISTRY` with a fixture-scoped object or a factory-returned dict. Remove the re-export from `tests/helpers/messages.py`.
3. **Move pairing cleanup into the factory module** (effort: 10 min) — Add an autouse fixture in `tests/factories/stores.py` itself to close and clear `_open_pairing_stores` / `_open_pairing_managers`, so cleanup is co-located with the state.
4. **Deduplicate `_make_ctx_mock`** (effort: 10 min) — Make `tests/fixtures/agent_harness.py` import `_make_ctx_mock` from `tests.factories.pools` instead of redefining it.
5. **Decide fate of `agent_harness`** (effort: 5 min) — Either delete the unused harness or migrate one integration test to use it; unused infrastructure rots silently.
6. **Align `discord_adapter` fixture with helper** (effort: 10 min) — Add `http` mock to `discord_adapter` fixture so it matches `make_dc_adapter` and is usable for `render_audio` tests.
7. **Remove deprecated shim** (effort: 5 min) — Delete `tests/fixtures/fake_drivers.py` and update the single import in `tests/fixtures/agent_harness.py` to use `tests.fakes` directly.
8. **Clean up temp dirs in bootstrap patches** (effort: 10 min) — Track `tempfile.mkdtemp()` results in a list and clean up in a fixture teardown, or use `tmp_path` / `TemporaryDirectory` context manager.
